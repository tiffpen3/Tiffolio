Challenge

  • Cleaning and formatting prospect lists was a fully manual process, taking several days of work per cycle and slowing down ABM campaign execution.
  • High-value travel operators needed specialized, high-touch campaigns to move through the sales pipeline.

Strategy

  • Built an AI-powered automation script in Google Sheets that automatically pulled and filled in missing account data, reducing list hygiene from several days to one hour.
  • Planned and executed 40+ ABM campaigns with custom messaging, automated direct outreach, and tailored landing assets targeting 100–500 prospect accounts per tier.

Outcome

  • Closed a major enterprise travel provider through a hyper-personalized 1:1 ABM sequence.
  • Campaigns drove installs to 164% of goal and nearly doubled the demo-to-install conversion rate, achieving a 6.8 ROAS against a 5.2 target.
